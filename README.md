

## Advanced Interactive Features

- Startup Evaluator: venture score, success potential estimate, funding readiness, risks and recommendations.
- Investor Matching: ranks investors using historical industry and geographic activity.
- VentureLens Agent: natural-language access to ecosystem analytics, investors, startups and forecasts.
- Dynamic Streamlit controls and chat-based interaction.

### Disclaimer
Startup evaluation scores are analytical estimates based on user-provided information and recorded ecosystem patterns. They are not guarantees of startup success or investment outcomes.
