# 🚀 VentureLens

### AI-Powered Startup Ecosystem Intelligence & Decision Support Platform

[![Live Demo](https://img.shields.io/badge/Live%20Demo-Streamlit-FF4B4B?logo=streamlit&logoColor=white)](https://venturelens-ndwamgacaumbbxxlwtn99m.streamlit.app/)
[![GitHub](https://img.shields.io/badge/GitHub-Repository-181717?logo=github)](https://github.com/blanushka004/VentureLens)

VentureLens is an interactive startup intelligence and decision-support platform built to analyze the Indian startup ecosystem.

It combines **PostgreSQL, ETL pipelines, multidimensional analytics, OLAP operations, funding forecasting, startup evaluation, investor intelligence, AI-assisted insights, and interactive reporting** in a Streamlit application.

Unlike a traditional static dashboard, VentureLens allows users to explore ecosystem data, compare startups and industries, analyze investors, forecast funding trends, ask analytical questions, and evaluate their own startup ideas.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Startup ecosystem data is often fragmented across funding reports, investor databases, market studies, and individual company records.

Founders, analysts, and investors may want to answer questions such as:

- Which industries attract the most funding?
- Which startup hubs are growing?
- Who are the most active investors?
- Which startups show strong funding momentum?
- How is startup funding changing over time?
- What could future funding activity look like?
- Which industries appear promising?
- How viable is a new startup idea based on its characteristics?

VentureLens brings these analyses into a single interactive platform.

## 🚀 Startup Evaluation Engine

One of the core decision-support features of VentureLens allows users to evaluate their own startup idea.

Users can provide details such as:

- Industry
- City
- Startup stage
- Team size
- Founder experience
- Revenue
- User traction
- Funding requirement
- Market size
- Competition level
- Business model

VentureLens generates an analytical assessment including:

- **Venture Potential Score**
- **Success Potential Estimate**
- **Funding Readiness**
- **Risk Assessment**
- **Strengths**
- **Weaknesses**
- **Recommended Actions**
- **Potential Investor Matches**

The assessment combines user-provided information with signals derived from the VentureLens startup ecosystem dataset.

> The evaluation is intended as a decision-support estimate and not a guarantee of startup success.
